Stacking order

Every piece is independently movable, including split-off parts of the same program. Floors is the only input -- Target NIA and NIA/floor are both computed from the program's fixed total, split proportionally by floor count. Assign bands yourself -- pieces sharing a band converge to that band's weighted-average NIA/floor, which is how split pieces end up with genuinely different floor plates.
